Our Story & Mission

From a small shop on Tariq Road to Karachi's most trusted footwear destination — this is the StepUp story.

How StepUp Shoes Began

StepUp Shoes was founded in 2012 by Muhammad Farhan, a passionate footwear enthusiast who grew up watching his father polish shoes in their small home in Liaquatabad, Karachi. Frustrated by the lack of affordable, quality shoes in Karachi markets, Farhan opened a tiny 12-foot shop on Tariq Road with just 30 pairs of shoes and a dream.

Word spread quickly. Customers came from PECHS, Defence, and Clifton — all searching for the same thing: well-made shoes at honest prices. By 2015, StepUp had expanded into a 2,000 sq. ft. store with over 500 styles of running shoes, formal shoes, and casual sneakers for men.

Today, StepUp Shoes serves over 10,000 customers annually across Karachi and ships to every major city in Pakistan. Our commitment remains unchanged — give every customer a pair of shoes they are proud to walk in.

Our Mission & Values

Everything we do at StepUp Shoes is guided by four core beliefs that have shaped us since day one.

🎯 Quality First

We stock only shoes that meet our strict quality standards. Every pair is inspected before it reaches your door — no compromises on comfort or durability.

🤝 Honest Pricing

Fair, transparent prices — no hidden charges, no bargaining theatre. We believe everyone in Karachi deserves premium footwear at honest prices.

🌎 Community Roots

StepUp Shoes is a Karachi business, built by Karachiites, for Karachiites. We hire locally, source responsibly, and give back to the community.

♻ Sustainable Future

We are actively reducing packaging waste and partnering with eco-conscious manufacturers to make premium footwear that is kinder to the planet.

Our Journey Through the Years

Key milestones in the StepUp Shoes story — from a tiny shop to a Karachi icon.

2012

The First Step

Muhammad Farhan opens the first StepUp Shoes store on Tariq Road, Karachi, with just 30 pairs and a vision for quality footwear.

2015

Expansion & Growth

Moved to a bigger 2,000 sq. ft. location. Launched the RS running shoes line — an instant hit with Karachi's fitness community.

2018

Going Digital

Launched WhatsApp ordering and social media presence on Instagram and Facebook, reaching customers across all neighbourhoods of Karachi.

2020

Nationwide Shipping

Launched nationwide delivery covering 25 cities. Home delivery in Karachi within 24 hours was introduced during the pandemic.

2024

10,000 Happy Customers

Celebrated serving our 10,000th customer. Launched the StepUp Loyalty Programme and the new formal shoes collection — Monaco and Oxford lines.
